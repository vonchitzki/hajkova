# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS (user's choice, 2026-10-27). Three self-contained homepage options, no build step, hand-off ready for any developer or site builder later.

## Users

Primary: brides-to-be and women commissioning a made-to-measure or ready-to-wear piece from a Czech fashion designer. They arrive from Instagram, a referral, or a magazine mention, usually on a phone, and are deciding whether to write to the designer. Secondary: stylists, editors, and press checking the designer's work and credentials.

## Product Purpose

The site is the designer's public home: it shows the work (bridal and collections), establishes credibility (press features), and turns interest into a conversation through the contact form. Success is a qualified enquiry sent from the homepage or the Contact page.

## Positioning

One named designer, Marketa Hajkova, whose own name is the brand; made-to-measure bridal and seasonal collections from a single atelier rather than a label or a shop. The work is the argument; the site must recede behind the photography.

## Operating Context

Site pages: Homepage, Bridal, Collections, About, Contact. Navigation carries the page links, the logotype "Marketa Hajkova", and an Instagram link rendered as an icon. The homepage sections in order: hero with background image and the name overlay; Bridal teaser (short description and image); Collections teaser (short description and image); carousel of media logos the designer was featured in; horizontally scrollable image gallery with arrows; contact form; footer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Copy language: Czech first (user's choice, 2026-10-27), with structure ready for an English version later (`lang` attribute, no text baked into images, labels centralized).
- Palette is pinned by the user: white, black, beige, grey only. No accent color.
- Visual register pinned by the user: minimalist, clean, nothing that reads as generated. References the user prefers: hanavaltova.com and nature-store-1.webflow.io.
- Contact form: static markup only, no backend yet (undecided: form handler/service).
- Gallery: horizontal scroll with previous/next arrows, must also work by touch and keyboard.
- Media carousel: logos only, no claims text.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and logotype: "Marketa Hajkova" (set in the site's type; no logo file exists yet).
- Instagram is the one social channel shown (URL undecided, placeholder `#`).
- User-pinned references and palette above are binding.

## Evidence on Hand

- No photography, logo files, press logos, or copy delivered yet (user's choice: placeholders for now, 2026-10-27). All photography in the options is stock placeholder, listed for replacement. Media logos are typeset placeholder names, not confirmed features; they must be replaced with the designer's real press list before launch.
- No testimonials, prices, or client names exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- The photograph is the content; chrome stays nearly invisible.
- One typographic voice per option, used with discipline; hierarchy from size and spacing, not ornament.
- Every section exists because the user listed it; nothing is added for decoration.
- Mobile is the first reading context (Instagram referrals), desktop the editorial one.
- Honest placeholders: everything synthetic is listed for replacement, never presented as fact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Czech diacritics must render in every chosen face (latin-ext coverage). Gallery and carousel must be operable by keyboard and respect reduced-motion preferences. Form fields carry visible labels.
