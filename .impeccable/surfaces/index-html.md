# Surface brief: index.html (homepage)

Scope: homepage of the Marketa Hajkova site, visitor mode Experience; the contact form is the one Persuade moment at the end.
Audience: brides and clients arriving from Instagram on a phone; editors on desktop. Job: see the work, believe in the designer, write.
Proof/content: placeholder stock photography (Unsplash), placeholder press logos (public-domain wordmarks from Wikimedia Commons), all listed in README.md for replacement. No claims invented.
Constraints pinned by the user: minimalist; white, black, beige, grey only; nav = pages + logotype "Marketa Hajkova" + Instagram icon; sections in order hero, Svatební, Kolekce, Media (logos), Galerie (press images) directly under Media, contact form, footer; Czech copy, English-ready; fonts Bebas Neue for headlines, Helvetica for body (user decision 2026-10-28).

User decision 2026-10-28: hero and navigation from option A (runway), everything below from option B (lookbook). The three exploratory options stay in options/ as archive.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A fashion-show opening on a lookbook body: the name as a condensed poster title across the full width of a monochrome hero, then quiet white plates. It refuses the centered-logo card stack.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, black ink, one hairline weight, paper-grey footer. Bebas Neue uppercase for every headline and the logotype, Helvetica Neue light for body and labels. Hero photo grayscale; plates and gallery muted color (saturate .6). No boxes, shadows, or radius except the circular gallery arrows.

STORY: The visitor sees the name as a title, the work as plates, the press as proof, and writes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed grayscale photo with top and bottom scrims. "MARKETA HAJKOVA" in Bebas Neue at about 15.4vw spanning the bottom edge, white. Hairline nav: page links left, logotype centered (visible once the nav turns solid), Instagram icon right. Primary action: "Kontakt" in the nav and the form at the end.

FORM: Runway hero (candidate 5 of 7, seed key a7a944c4) merged with the lookbook body (candidate 1) by user decision.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
