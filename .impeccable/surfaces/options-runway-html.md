# Surface brief: options/runway.html (homepage, option A)

Scope: homepage of the Marketa Hajkova site, visitor mode Experience (the work leads; the contact form is the one Persuade moment at the end).
Audience: brides and clients arriving from Instagram on a phone; editors on desktop. Job: see the work, believe in the designer, write.
Proof/content: placeholder stock photography and typeset placeholder media names, all listed for replacement. No claims invented.
Constraints pinned by the user: minimalist; white, black, beige, grey only; nav = pages + logotype "Marketa Hajkova" + Instagram icon; sections in the user's order; Czech copy, English-ready.

Three options are built side by side at the user's request: A runway (assigned by the roll), B lookbook (Impeccable's pick, candidate 1), C atelier (candidate 2). The user locks one afterwards; the other two files are then deleted or archived.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a fashion show: one procession of looks that moves left to right, the designer's name set as the show title. It refuses the centered-logo lookbook stack of same-size image-and-text cards.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, black ink, a single hairline rule weight. Bodoni Moda for the name and section titles, Hanken Grotesk for every small label. Photography monochrome. No boxes, shadows, or rounded corners; black fields own whole regions (press strip, contact, footer).

STORY: The visitor watches the work walk past, believes this is a designer with a point of view, and writes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed photo. "Marketa Hajkova" in Bodoni at about 13vw, white, spanning the bottom edge. Hairline nav at top: page links left, logotype hidden on the home hero (the title is the logotype), Instagram icon right. Primary action: "Kontakt" in the nav and the form at the end.

FORM: The runway, candidate 5 of 7 on the ordered grounded list; seed key a7a944c4.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Roll verdicts (seed a7a944c4)

All six challengers declined on both axes against the pinned minimalist brief (wax pack, console void, cloud quarry, silk canopy, split-flap board, arcade pixel). Raises kept:
- from split-flap concourse: the gallery is one ruled row with fixed cells, nothing floats; arrows and counter live on the rule.
- from wax pack: every image carries a one-line caption in a fixed position, like a card's name strip.
- from console void: black regions are whole rooms, never a dark card on white.
